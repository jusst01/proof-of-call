Proof of Call v2

Don’t trust callers. Verify them.

Alpha callers delete their misses, edit their calls, and screenshot only the wins. Proof of Call ends that: a caller registers a call on-chain, before the move — the block timestamp can’t be forged. An oracle fetches the entry price from DexScreener (the caller never types it) and scores the call at 15m / 1h / 6h / 24h. Wins and losses stay on the record, forever.

In v2 a call can carry a target, an invalidation price and a short thesis, an optional 0.1 NEAR bond that is forfeited if the call loses ≥50% at 24h — and callers can link their trading wallets, so the record can show whether the wallet actually bought.

📣 Register a call

What happens next: the contract stores your account + the block timestamp and mints you a receipt NFT (call-<id>). You never enter an entry price — the oracle fetches the entry price from DexScreener and writes it, then records the outcome at 15m, 1h, 6h and 24h, including whether your target or invalidation price was hit. If you attached the bond, it stays locked until the 24h score lands. A short profits when the price falls; a long when it rises. The record cannot be edited or deleted — by anyone.

🔍 How it works — and how to verify everything

  1. Register. The caller signs register_call(chain, token_ref, direction, target_price, invalidation_price, thesis), optionally attaching the 0.1 NEAR bond. The contract stores the signer and the block timestamp. That pair is the proof the call existed, then.
  2. Entry price. Set only by the oracle, fetched from DexScreener at registration time and stored verbatim — compare it against the public source tick-for-tick.
  3. Scoring. At fixed horizons — 15m / 1h / 6h / 24h, no discretion — the oracle records the exit price (verbatim), the return in basis points from the caller’s perspective, and whether the call’s target / invalidation prices were hit. One outcome per horizon, append-only.
  4. Bond. A bonded call locks 0.1 NEAR. After the 24h score the bond is claimable back by the caller (claim_bond) — unless the call lost ≥50% at 24h, in which case it is forfeited.
  5. Linked wallets. A caller can link trading wallets (link_wallet / unlink_wallet); the oracle checks them against the call and records a plain-language verdict on the call itself.
  6. Receipt. Every call mints one receipt NFT to the caller at registration: portable proof of when the call was made.
  7. Recompute it yourself. Every number on this page is a view call against one public contract. Take any stored entry/exit price pair and the direction — the return is arithmetic anyone can redo.
